Queued — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Queued ("the app") is a video game backlog tracker developed by Serkan Tahtakaya. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The short version: your backlog stays on your device, and we collect as little as possible.

Data stored on your device

Your game list, statuses, ratings, notes, custom lists and preferences are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier, purchase history for this app, and basic device information (such as device model, OS version, and locale). We never see your name, Apple ID, or payment details.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we use OneSignal to deliver them. OneSignal receives a push token, an anonymous identifier, basic device information, and app-related tags (such as how many games are in your backlog) used to time and personalize notifications. You can turn every notification type off in the app's settings or disable notifications entirely in iOS Settings.

Game artwork and metadata

Game covers, genres and release information are loaded from third-party game databases (such as IGDB). When artwork is loaded, your device's IP address is visible to the provider serving the images, as with any content loaded from the internet.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not show third-party ads based on your personal data. We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, or location. The app contains no third-party analytics SDKs beyond the services listed above.

Children

Queued is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear all locally stored data at any time from Settings → Clear all data. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. You can withdraw notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.
